Complex Data Analytics

A text-analytics course rebuilt for the age of large language models.

Text is now the dominant form of business data — reviews, tickets, contracts, transcripts, social posts. This course takes students from classical natural language processing through large language models, with evaluation as the constant, and puts the emphasis on judgment: matching a method to the decision, the budget, and the stakes, and knowing when a language model is the wrong tool.
