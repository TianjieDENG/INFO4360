Group project

Pick a method. Defend it with evidence.

Groups of two or three work across the whole quarter on a single question: given a real business text dataset, what is the right way to analyze it — and how do you know?

What makes it different

Groups do not simply build one model and report its accuracy. They place their approach on the ladder of approaches, compare it against at least one lighter alternative and one heavier one — including “just prompt a large model” — and back the final choice with evaluation evidence rather than intuition. The deliverable is a decision, argued.

Two phases

Phase I — framing and baseline

A business question stated as a text task, the dataset and how it was obtained, a working baseline model, and a concrete plan for how the group will evaluate competing approaches.

Phase II — comparison and recommendation

A full comparison across rungs of the ladder, a recommendation written for a decision-maker, and an honest account of where the chosen method fails and what that costs.

Logistics

Team formation, datasets, deliverable formats, due dates, and the grading rubric are posted in Canvas. Late work on the project is accepted with a penalty; see the syllabus.
