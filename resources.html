Readings

A sample of the papers the course is built around. The full list, with any additions for the current term, is in Canvas.

  • Pang, Lee & Vaithyanathan (2002). Thumbs up? Sentiment Classification using Machine Learning Techniques. EMNLP.
  • Hutto & Gilbert (2014). VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Text. ICWSM.
  • Ghose & Ipeirotis (2011). Estimating the Helpfulness and Economic Impact of Product Reviews. IEEE TKDE.
  • Vaswani et al. (2017). Attention Is All You Need. NeurIPS.
  • Devlin et al. (2019). BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding. NAACL.
  • Wang et al. (2018). GLUE: A Multi-Task Benchmark and Analysis Platform for Natural Language Understanding. EMNLP workshop.

Datasets

  • Movie reviews

    Labeled positive/negative review text for sentiment classification. Available via Hugging Face.

  • Social media posts

    Short, informal, noisy text — where lexicon methods start to break. Available from various sources on Hugging Face.

  • Product reviews

    Free text alongside ratings and helpfulness votes, for prediction and extraction. Amazon reviews available on Hugging Face.

  • Short-term rental listings and reviews

    Listing descriptions and guest reviews joined to structured attributes and price. Airbnb and similar datasets on Hugging Face.

Getting up to speed

Python and pandas refreshers are posted in Canvas. The prerequisite is STAT 4610; if your programming is rusty, work through the refreshers before the first hands-on session.
